A complete architectural handbook targeted at engineering teams transitionary from monolithic layouts to distributed service structures.

### Key Content Areas
- **Consistency Patterns**: Detailed breakdowns of the Saga pattern (both choreography and orchestration), two-phase commits, and outbox tables.
- **Resilience**: Implementing retry back-offs, circuit breakers, and dead-letter queue structures.
- **Observability**: Guidelines on distributed trace correlation, standard tags, and structured logging formats.
